Program

Thursday, 3 December 2026

AID 2026 is a half-day workshop, held as a parallel session of IEEE PRDC 2026. Nine peer-reviewed papers are presented in two sessions. Each presentation is allotted a 20-minute slot, structured as a 15-minute talk followed by a 5-minute Q&A session.
